About me

20 years of clinical practice in Brazil

I'm Filipe Starling, a clinical psychologist based in Brasília, Brazil. I trained at the Universidade de Brasília (UnB) and the Universidade Católica de Brasília (UCB), and have spent the last two decades working primarily with couples and individuals navigating non-traditional relationships.

I studied sexology with Regina Navarro Lins, one of the most prominent Brazilian researchers in the field, and I'm a trained practitioner in EMDR and Brainspotting — modalities particularly useful when working with jealousy, attachment wounds, and relational trauma.

I wrote Não Monogamia Responsável, the leading book in Portuguese on ethical non-monogamy, polyamory, and the cultural construction of monogamy. The book is rated 4.7 on Amazon Brazil and has shaped public conversation on the topic, with appearances in major Brazilian media.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does payment work from abroad?

Most international clients use Wise (formerly TransferWise) or PayPal to send payment in their local currency. The conversion to BRL happens at the platform's exchange rate — usually within 1-2% of the mid-market rate. Brazilian clients abroad with a Brazilian bank account can use PIX instantly. I'll send specific instructions when we schedule.

How do time zones work?

I'm based in Brasília (BRT, UTC−3). I work mornings and evenings local time, which generally works well for clients in the Americas (same or adjacent time zones) and is doable for Europe (my morning = their afternoon). For Asia/Pacific, we can schedule my early morning or late night.

Is my Brazilian psychology license valid for clients abroad?

I'm licensed by the Conselho Federal de Psicologia (CFP) in Brazil. International tele-therapy regulations vary by country — for most clients abroad, accessing therapy from a Brazilian psychologist is legal as the service is provided from Brazil. I'm happy to discuss this in your specific case during our intro call.

Do you work with individuals, or only couples?

Both. About half of my practice is individual therapy (often related to relationship questions but not exclusively — anxiety, identity, sexuality, trauma). The other half is couples and polycules, sometimes with 3+ people in the session.

What if my partner doesn't speak English?

I can conduct sessions in mixed Portuguese-English when needed — useful for binational couples. Just let me know in advance and we'll plan accordingly.

How is this different from BetterHelp / Talkspace?

Those platforms match you with a generalist therapist for short-form chat-based support. My practice is specialized (relationships, sexuality, non-monogamy), session-based (50 min deep work, not chat), and I personally see every client across the full arc of therapy.

How do we get started?

Send me a WhatsApp message (English or Portuguese) telling me briefly what you're looking for. I'll reply within 24 hours with availability and we can schedule a 20-minute free intro call to see if we're a good fit before committing to a full session.

Ready to start?

The intro call is free, 20 minutes, and there's no commitment to continue. Just an honest conversation about what you're looking for and whether I'm the right fit.
